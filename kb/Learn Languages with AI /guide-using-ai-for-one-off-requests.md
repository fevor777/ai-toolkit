# Guide "Using AI for One-off Requests"

The one-off uses in this section are perfect for single-time self-study moments when you need a quick explanation, correction, or practice session - without needing to follow a full lesson or learning path.

As you read the guide, use an LLM of your choice (ChatGPT, Dial, Claude, etc.). This way, you can immediately try out the prompts that are relevant for you.

## What You Can Do

| What you can do | Suggested prompt |
|---|---|
| 📚 Translate and explain a word or phrase | What does "break the ice" mean? Explain in plain English and give 3 examples. |
| 🔍 Check a sentence for grammar or clarity | Check this sentence for mistakes and suggest corrections: "He go to work yesterday". |
| ✏️ Rephrase a sentence to sound more natural | How can I say this more naturally: "I want to offer answers how we can work with more productivity". Suggest 5 options. |
| 📄 Shorten your text and keep it correct and easy to understand | Shorten and rewrite in plain English, make it concise and easy to understand: [paste the text into the prompt]. |
| 📊 Create a vocabulary list | Give me 5 useful phrases that I can use at a daily meeting as a Software Engineer. Add explanations and examples. |
| 💡 Generate examples for grammar rules | Show me 5 example sentences using the passive voice. |
| 🎓 Choose the right word or expression when you're unsure | Which is better: "make a decision" or "do a decision"? Why? |
| 🎭 Roleplay a mini-dialogue | Can we do a short roleplay where I'm ordering food at a restaurant? |
| 🧠 Quiz me on a topic when I want to practice | Ask me 5 questions to practice past simple tense. |

## 💡 Useful Tips

- 🗨️ If the response you get is unclear, ask the AI to explain it differently, more simply, or give more examples.
- 🗣️ When you get a response you like and would like to use, say it out loud. It helps you remember better, speak more fluently, and build confidence using the new language.
- 📝 Even if it's a one-time question, save helpful responses (in a notebook, app, or document). This way, you can return to them and use them later.
